Once a subscription fee is paid for premium features, it is NON-REFUNDABLE and NON-TRANSFERABLE. We do not provide refunds for:

Refund may only be considered in exceptional cases: (a) Duplicate payment due to technical error, proven by bank statement, (b) Service was completely non-functional for more than 72 continuous hours due to our fault. Such requests must be made within 7 days of payment with proof to our support email.
