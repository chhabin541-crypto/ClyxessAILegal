1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and keep you logged in securely. Cookies do not give us access to your device, your files, or your personal passwords.

Our Service is strictly for parents and legal guardians who are 18 years or older. Only a parent or legal guardian can create and manage an account on Clyxess AI. Children cannot create an account or login directly. All cookies are stored only on the parent's device, not on a child's device independently. Parents are fully responsible for managing cookies and account security on their device.

4. No Sale of Cookie Data

We do NOT sell, rent, or share data collected via cookies with any advertiser or data broker. Cookie data is used solely for functionality and security. We do NOT use cookies to collect your child's personal data without parental consent. We do NOT use cookies to train AI models with your personal data.

5. How to Control Cookies - Your Control

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies. However, if you block our essential authentication cookies, you will not be able to log in to Clyxess AI, as login requires Supabase session cookies. This is necessary for functionality.

  • Browser Settings: You can block or delete cookies anytime from your browser settings - Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge.
  • Logout: On logout, your login session cookie will be deleted automatically.
  • Delete Account: On account deletion, all cookies and data linked to your account will be removed as per our Privacy Policy.
